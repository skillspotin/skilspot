User Guide & Documentation

Thank you for choosing Skillspot Studio. This professional AI tool is designed for high-quality Malayalam voice production.

1. Quick Start Guide (Installation)

  • 1 Move the entire downloaded folder to your Desktop or Documents.
  • 2 Double-click Skillspot_Studio_PRO.exe.
  • 3 If Windows shows a "Security Warning," click "More Info" and then "Run Anyway."
  • 4 The software will automatically generate a file named paste_script_here.txt.

2. How to Use

  1. Open paste_script_here.txt and paste your Malayalam script.
    Note: Line 1 is the folder name; Line 2 is the script.
  2. Select your preferred Voice Character in the software interface.
  3. Use the Pitch Slider to adjust the voice energy (Younger/Older).
  4. Click 'Test Voice Sample' to verify your internet connection and audio.
  5. Click 'Generate Full Project' to create your MP3 file.

Important Requirement

An active internet connection is required for AI voice generation to work.
